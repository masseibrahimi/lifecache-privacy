LifeCache Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

LifeCache ("the app") is a personal memory app for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information.

The short version

Everything you save in LifeCache stays on your phone. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send your information anywhere. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There are no ads, no accounts and no analytics.

What the app stores, and where

  • Memories you create: text you type or dictate, photos you take or choose, and the reminders you set. They are stored in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Text and objects recognized in photos: LifeCache reads text in your photos and labels what they show using on-device machine learning (Google ML Kit, bundled in the app). This processing happens entirely on your device.
  • Approximate location (optional, off by default): if you turn on "Remember where I saved things", the app asks for approximate location permission and stores the place with each new memory. You can turn it off at any time in Settings, and you can delete any memory.

Voice input

When you speak a memory, LifeCache uses your phone's built-in speech recognition service (for example, Google voice typing). That service is provided by your phone's system, not by LifeCache, and its handling of audio is covered by that provider's privacy policy. LifeCache only receives the resulting text. The app asks the service to work offline where the phone supports it.

Backups and sharing

LifeCache excludes its data from Android cloud backups and device-to-device transfers. Your information only leaves the app when you choose to: by sharing a memory to another app, or by exporting your memories to a file you pick.

Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications: to show reminders and the optional morning summary.
  • Camera (through your camera app): only when you choose to take a photo.
  • Approximate location: only if you turn on place tagging.
  • Biometric / screen lock: only if you turn on app lock.
  • Run at startup: so reminders still fire after your phone restarts.

Deleting your data

You can delete any memory, use auto-delete, or use "Delete everything" in Settings. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data from your device.

Children

LifeCache is not directed at children under 13.
